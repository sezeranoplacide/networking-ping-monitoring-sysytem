Network Connection Guidance

  • Verify each device IP is correct and reachable from this server.
  • Ensure the device is on the same network or that ICMP/ping traffic is allowed through firewalls.
  • Use a wired connection for critical infrastructure, and assign static IPs to key devices.
  • Group devices by location, function, or VLAN to quickly identify offline segments.
  • Confirm device credentials and management interfaces are accessible for troubleshooting.
